Kenya's Cyberbullying Laws & Legal Action

Computer Misuse and Cybercrimes Act, 2018

Covers cyber harassment, cyberstalking, impersonation, and threatening online messages.

Data Protection Act, 2019

Protects personal information from unauthorized sharing.

Children's Protection

Protects children from online abuse and exploitation.

Possible Legal Penalties

Courts may impose fines, imprisonment, content removal, and protection orders depending on the offence.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is cyberbullying?

Using digital platforms to harass or intimidate someone.

What should I do if I'm being cyberbullied?

Save evidence, block the bully, report the account, and tell a trusted adult.

Can I report anonymously?

Yes, your name and email are optional.

Is cyberbullying illegal in Kenya?

Yes. Some forms are offences under Kenyan law.

Who can help me?

Your school, parents, counselors, the Communications Authority of Kenya, or emergency services if you are in immediate danger.
